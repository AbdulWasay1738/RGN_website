# **Jenna Colombo \| The Person You Call When It\'s Time to Grow**

Some people are good at talking about business growth. Jenna Colombo has spent her career in the middle of it.

She has built companies from zero, helped founders raise millions, opened doors that seemed closed, and brought the right people together to make things happen. She knows what it looks like when a business has a great product, a founder with a big vision, and a serious gap between what the company can do and what the market understands about it.

Jenna co-founded Groundswell Startups and built it from the ground up into the Southeast\'s largest privately funded technology incubator. The companies in its network went on to raise more than \$65 million and generate over \$162 million in exits. At Helicon Technologies, she helped raise \$2.5 million in less than nine months, organized more than 150 investor meetings, recruited executive talent, and helped build a B2B sales pipeline.

Those numbers matter. But the real story is how she gets results. She knows how to find the right people, tell a story that makes them pay attention, build trust, and turn a conversation into an opportunity.

Today, through Reel Good News, Jenna brings that experience to businesses that need their expertise to translate into growth. She helps founders turn what they know, what they have built, and what their customers have achieved into a year of content that gives buyers a reason to believe in the business before the first sales call.

Because more content is not the goal. More trust is. Better sales conversations are. A business that can explain its value, prove its results, and earn the right customers is the goal.

Jenna brings the perspective of an operator, the instincts of a communicator, and the drive of someone who has built things from scratch. She does not start by asking what the company should post on social media. She starts by asking where the business wants to go, what is getting in the way, and what needs to happen to get there.

If the business is ready to grow but the market has not caught up to its potential, Jenna knows how to help close that gap.
