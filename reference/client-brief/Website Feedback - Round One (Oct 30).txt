CHANGE #9 - CONCISE COPY


Remove unnecessary words from all headlines, subheadlines, and lists. Prioritize concise, direct

language that communicates value quickly. Eliminate redundant phrases, filler words, and

unnecessary explanations. Every word should serve a clear purpose.


Avoid overusing second-person language, particularly the words “you” and “your.” Lead with the

value, outcome, or benefit rather than repeatedly addressing the reader directly. Use strong,

declarative statements instead.


For example, replace:


“You get a recommendation and a proposal that fits. We will send your top 10 quick wins.”


With:


“Receive a tailored recommendation and 10 quick wins.”


Keep copy tight, specific, professional, and easy to scan. Do not sacrifice clarity or substance

for brevity.
