# **Reel Good News**

## **Company Summary**

Reel Good News helps businesses turn their expertise, customer results, and market knowledge into a year of strategic content captured in one filming day. The company combines go to market strategy, customer research, storytelling, and video production to build trust with high intent buyers and support sales. Its priority is to serve technology companies and other businesses with complex sales processes, limited marketing capacity, and a need to explain why their solutions matter. Rather than producing content for visibility alone, Reel Good News connects every content asset to customer needs, business objectives, and measurable commercial outcomes.

## **What Works**

The one day filming model gives busy founders a practical alternative to constant content production. Jenna brings experience supporting entrepreneurs, developing go to market strategies, and translating complex ideas into accessible stories. The emphasis on customer problems, expert perspectives, and documented results creates opportunities to build credibility. Existing relationships across Florida\'s entrepreneurial ecosystem provide a starting point for customer acquisition.

## **What Needs Work**

The ideal customer profile needs tighter definition, and the core offer needs validation through paid engagements. The promise of 365 days of content requires a documented production process, Reelistic deliverable specifications, and clear distribution expectations. The company also needs quantified case studies, a repeatable sales process, defined pricing and margins, and a measurement framework connecting content to qualified leads, sales opportunities, and revenue.

# **Growth Strategy Questionnaire**

## **1. Why This Matters**

### **What specific problem do you solve?**

Reel Good News solves the problem of businesses needing to communicate consistently with prospective customers but lacking the time, resources, or internal process to produce useful content. Founders are responsible for running the company, developing relationships, and closing sales. Marketing teams often struggle to get executive input, secure approvals, and produce content that addresses actual customer concerns. Reel Good News uses research, strategy, and a concentrated filming day to build a library of content designed to educate buyers, establish credibility, and support the sales process.

### **Who feels this problem the most?**

Founders and CEOs of growing businesses feel the problem most directly because their time is divided between operations, sales, partnerships, hiring, and growth. Heads of marketing and business development also experience it when they are expected to maintain a consistent online presence without sufficient access to leadership, subject matter experts, or customer evidence. The strongest initial customers are likely to be companies with complex offerings and long sales cycles, where a lack of clear communication makes it harder to build trust.

### **Why do they care about solving it?**

Inconsistent or generic content can make a capable business appear less credible than it is. When buyers cannot easily understand a product, its relevance, or the evidence behind its claims, sales teams must spend more time explaining the same information. Strategic content gives buyers a way to evaluate the business before a sales conversation, helps sales teams answer recurring questions, and gives prospective customers reasons to engage.

### **How are they solving it today?**

Businesses often rely on occasional filming, fReelancers, internal marketing staff, generic social media posts, paid advertising, or the founder\'s personal efforts. Some hire agencies that focus on posting frequency or production rather than the business questions their content should answer. Reel Good News can replace fragmented efforts with a planned system, provided the research, production, approval, and distribution process is clearly defined.

### **Are there enough people with this problem who would actually pay to fix it?**

The opportunity is plausible, particularly among established B2B companies that have the budget to invest in marketing and the complexity to benefit from customer education. However, the size of the paying market has not yet been established through documented sales. Customer interviews, paid pilots, proposal conversion rates, and willingness to renew should determine whether the target segment is commercially attractive.

### **Is your solution one-time or recurring?**

The initial filming engagement can be sold as a defined project, while content distribution, performance reviews, campaign development, and marketing team training can create recurring revenue. The recommended model is a paid strategy and production engagement followed by an optional monthly or quarterly support agreement. Recurring services should be offered only when clients receive continuing value that justifies the cost.

### **Why is your solution need to have versus nice to have?**

The business need is effective communication with prospective customers, not simply more social media posts. Companies with complex products and long sales cycles must explain their value, establish credibility, and address buyer concerns. Reel Good News becomes a higher priority when it can demonstrate that its content supports qualified opportunities, improves sales conversations, or reduces the time employees spend repeatedly explaining the offering. Without that evidence, content production remains easier for a buyer to postpone.
